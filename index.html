Information and Help

Welcome to the 3D Aim Trainer!

This application is designed to help you improve your aiming skills through various training exercises and scenarios.

How to Use the Training Library:

  • Select a skill type (Accuracy, Flicking, Tracking) from the Training Library.
  • Choose a mode and difficulty level that suits your needs.
  • Click "Start" to begin the training session.

Pause Menu:

  • During a training session, press the "Pause" button to access the pause menu.
  • You can resume, reset, or return to the Training Library from here.

Settings:

  • Customize your crosshair appearance and user settings in the Settings menu.
  • Adjust sensitivity and field of view (FOV) to your preference.

Stats:

  • View your overall accuracy, total targets hit, shots fired, and time played in the Stats section.
